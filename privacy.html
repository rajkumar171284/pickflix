PickFlix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Applies to PickFlix for Android (Google Play), package com.rajlabs.pickflix, version 3.0.0

Summary: PickFlix does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. There is no login, no account, no ads, no analytics and no payments. Your favourites and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

PickFlix is developed by Rajkumar Arumugam, Chennai, India ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. PickFlix has no user accounts or sign-in, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not operate any server that receives data from the app.

Data stored on your device

Your watchlist/favourites, watched list, ratings, recently viewed titles, reminders, language and other preferences are saved only in the app's local storage (WebView localStorage) on your device. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. If Android device backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include this app data in your own Google backup, under your Google account's settings.

Third-party content services

To show movie and series details, posters and cast photos, the app requests content directly from these services. Like any website, they automatically receive standard request data such as your IP address, device/browser user-agent and the content requested. PickFlix does not send them any personal identifiers.

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) – api.themoviedb.org, image.tmdb.org. TMDB privacy policy.
  • Wikimedia / Wikipedia – upload.wikimedia.org, en.wikipedia.org (some posters and info). Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • YouTube – trailers via www.youtube-nocookie.com / www.youtube.com and thumbnails from i.ytimg.com. Google privacy policy.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Links to other apps and sites

When you tap a streaming service (for example Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, ZEE5, SonyLIV, Sun NXT, aha and others), a ticketing service (for example BookMyShow, District, Ticketnew) or YouTube, PickFlix simply opens that third party's app or website. Anything you do there is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not this one. On Android, the app declares that it may check whether the BookMyShow app is installed so it can open it directly; this check stays on your device.

Permissions

  • INTERNET – needed to load movie information, images and trailers.

PickFlix requests no other permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or phone access.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Children

PickFlix is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Network requests to the content services above use HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
